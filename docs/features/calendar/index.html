Calendar and reminders

Keep the next commitment in view.

Updated 2026-08-10macOS 14+

What it does

Gojo can place calendar and reminder information among the glanceable tools in the notch workspace.

Why it is useful

It is useful when you need a quick check of what is next without opening Calendar or losing the context of the app you are working in.

How Calendar and reminders works in Gojo

  • Calendar information in the notch workspace
  • Reminder-related glanceable information
  • Can be kept out of the way when not needed

Good to know

Calendar and reminder availability depends on the permissions and accounts configured in macOS.
