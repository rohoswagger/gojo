Shortcuts

Keep your chosen actions close to the rest of your Mac tools.

Updated 2026-08-10macOS 14+

What it does

Gojo can surface shortcuts as part of the customizable set of tools in the notch workspace.

Why it is useful

It is useful when a repeatable action belongs next to media, files, windows, and other controls you reach for throughout the day.

How Shortcuts works in Gojo

  • Part of Gojo’s customizable tool selection
  • Keeps chosen actions close to the notch workspace
  • Can be hidden when it does not match your workflow

Good to know

The exact actions available depend on the shortcuts configured on your Mac.

One workspace, not another standalone utility

Shortcuts is one part of Gojo’s MacBook-notch workspace. Keep the tools you use visible, hide the rest, and reach media, windows, clipboard, files, display controls, and more from the same native surface.
