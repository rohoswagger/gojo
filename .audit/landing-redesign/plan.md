Done means the homepage builds, links remain correct, trial and purchase actions are easy to find, every product claim is supported, and live browser checks pass at desktop and mobile widths. Conversion lift remains unverified until real traffic is measured.

Scope is homepage presentation and homepage measurement readiness. Existing native-app work is outside scope. No dependency additions, deployment, analytics configuration writes, or checkout changes.

Throughput checkpoint. Analytics access is the uncertain dependency. Investigate independently while preparing page composition. Use one homepage implementation owner and separate read-only funnel research. Stop local work after build, link checks, browser review, and a bounded correction pass.

Structure candidates. A compact product stage leads with a trial action and price, followed by explorable screenshots, dictation proof, pricing, and FAQ. B editorial walkthrough leads with a single large screenshot and sequential feature stories. Grade on message clarity, above-fold action, evidence, mobile access, and fewer client boundaries. Prefer A if it makes product breadth understandable without the six-section scroll.
