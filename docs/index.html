Everything below is the real app, running in the space around the notch. Scroll once and you have seen the whole product.

Dictation

Talk. Gojo types. Nothing leaves your Mac.

Hold one shortcut and speak into whatever text field you are already in — Mail, Slack, a commit message, a search box. The words appear where your cursor is.

Recognition runs on a model you download once and keep. No API key, no account, no audio leaving the machine, and it still works on a plane.

  • Hold ⌃⌥ to talk, release to insert
  • Choose your model, or swap it later
  • Runs offline, on-device, every time

Media

Your music, without covering your work.

Artwork, title, and a scrubber sit in the notch. Skip, shuffle, and seek from whatever app is playing, without raising a single window.

  • Follows your current media source
  • Reorder the controls you actually use

Clipboard

The thing you copied five minutes ago.

Every copy is kept and searchable from the notch, so you stop re-copying the same link. Anything a supported password manager marks private is skipped outright.

  • Search without opening another app
  • Passwords and secrets stay out of history

Windows

See the window before you switch to it.

A switcher that previews what you are about to land on, plus a snap grid with the shortcut printed under every layout — so you learn them by using them.

  • Replaces ⌘⇥ with per-window previews
  • Halves, thirds, maximize, and zoom

Shelf

Put a file down without losing it.

Drag files up to the notch and they wait there while you change folders, desktops, or apps. Drag them back out when you have arrived — or hand them straight to AirDrop.

  • Survives folder, Space, and app switches
  • AirDrop target built into the shelf

Night Shift

Warm the screen when the day winds down.

Night Shift on your own schedule, with a toggle in the notch instead of a trip through System Settings. Sunset times are worked out on your Mac from a location you set once.

  • Starts with your Mac, if you want it to
  • Location is used locally and never sent anywhere
